Founder

Alexander Melhem

"I started MELHEM after fifteen years designing equipment for aerospace and medical fields. I noticed that the objects we rely on for critical tasks were either purely utilitarian or purely decorative.

There was a space between: objects that perform essential functions with uncompromising reliability, while embodying the restraint and quality of true luxury.

Our clients are individuals who make decisions affecting many lives and substantial resources. They need tools that won't fail, that communicate seriousness, and that don't distract. That's what we build."

Former lead engineer at Kronos Aerospace
Advisor to multiple security and logistics firms
